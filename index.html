THE QUESTION HAS CHANGED

Passing the exam is one thing.
Knowing what to do next is another.

AI can explain a topic, draft a design or generate code in minutes. That doesn’t make your learning pointless—but it does change what you need to practise. Can you solve a new problem, explain your decisions, work with people and use the tools well? That’s the gap SprintWorks is here to help you think through.

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Is the first conversation paid?

The introductory webinar on 28 November 2026 is free. Any separate advisory or cohort fees will be stated clearly before you agree to anything.

Is the January cohort open for enrolment?

Not yet. The first cohort is planned to begin on 28 January 2027. Curriculum, duration, format and pricing are still being finalised; joining the interest list is not a purchase or commitment.

Can a parent reach out on behalf of a student?

Yes. Choose “Parent / guardian” in the form and tell us what decision your family is working through. We encourage student participation in the conversation wherever appropriate.

Does SprintWorks guarantee a job or admission?

No. We provide guidance to support informed decisions; outcomes depend on many factors beyond any one advisor or programme.
